# Calculation rules

This is the behavioral contract for the Core calculation module. Annual values are data, not constants scattered through the application. The source review and remaining verification work are recorded in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

## Representation and output

| ID | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| C01 | Store monetary amounts as integer minor units with a currency code and exponent; EUR uses cents. Do not assume every foreign currency has two decimal places. Use exact decimal/rational arithmetic for FX and distance. EUR rounding is half away from zero at the final conversion or percentage operation. |
| C02 | Store instants plus IANA zones; preserve local dates relevant to the itinerary. Calculate elapsed absence from instants, not a subtraction of wall-clock displays. Use local calendar dates for day types and rate years. Reject overlapping trips; touching intervals are allowed. |
| C03 | Return inputs, effective date, rate value/source/revision, stable rule ID, intermediate amounts, final amounts and warnings for each calculation line. The frontend displays server results. |
| C04 | Use the travel day's year for meal/lodging rates and the journey's date for mileage. Missing or invalid required rate data is a blocker. Reuse the inherited CSVs in data/README.md; synthetic comparison fixtures do not replace them. Use overrides in drafts, with revision and reason; snapshots retain the values used at submission. |

## Meal allowance

The domestic baseline is EUR 14 for a qualifying partial day and EUR 28 for a full day. One-day travel must exceed eight hours; exactly eight hours does not qualify. Arrival/departure days of qualifying travel with an overnight stay receive the partial rate without an hours threshold; intervening full days receive the full rate. These central rules were checked against [§ 9(4a) EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__9.html).

- **C05:** A trip crossing midnight without an overnight stay can earn one partial allowance if total absence exceeds eight hours. Assign it to the date with greater absence; the baseline tie-break is the later date. Record actual lodging/in-transit status explicitly rather than inferring an overnight stay from midnight alone.
- **C06:** Across all of one user's trips and employers, calculate at most one meal allowance per calendar day. Aggregate nonoverlapping one-day absences when needed. When several day entitlements compete, use the highest eligible entitlement. Combine provided-meal flags once per meal, and count each contribution only once. Attribute an unpaid daily result to the eligible editable trip ending latest; ties use stable trip identity. Explain zero amounts assigned to other trips.
- **C07:** Subtract amounts already frozen for that day in submitted/paid claims. Allocate only the nonnegative remainder to editable trips. If existing frozen amounts exceed the recalculated entitlement, block submission until the affected claim(s) are corrected. A preview must include relevant trips outside the selected claim/period. Recheck within submission to prevent concurrent double payment.
- **C08:** For a foreign day, select the rate location last reached before local midnight; when returning to Germany, use the last foreign business location where applicable. Named city rates take precedence over a country's general rate. Apply the official annual table's territory/fallback mappings. A transit stop is not automatically a business location. Keep arrival times, zones and business-activity flags.
- **C09:** Allow a user-confirmed location override with reason. Special flight/ship rules and itineraries crossing the international date line must either have verified automated handling or require a confirmed day-by-day schedule of dates, locations and day types with reasons. An unresolved schedule blocks submission; silently applying a domestic/default rate is unacceptable. Automated handling of these cases is outside the minimal Core.
- **C10:** Deduct 20% of that day's full-day rate for provided breakfast and 40% each for lunch/dinner. Reduce each deduction by the contribution for that meal, floored at zero. Floor the remaining daily allowance at zero. Apply the day's rate even when the meal was provided elsewhere. A provided meal without an allowance creates a payroll-review notice, not a negative allowance. Source: [§ 9(4a), sentences 8–10 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__9.html).
- **C11:** Model stable work-location identity and an explicit per-day meal-allowance exclusion with reason. Warn when recorded attendance spans more than three months without a recorded interruption of at least four weeks. Explain that this is a screening warning, not an eligibility determination; let the user confirm the assessment or exclude the affected days before submission. Apply exclusion after meal deductions. Detailed attendance-frequency heuristics are deferred. Source: [§ 9(4a), sentences 6–7 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__9.html).

## Lodging and transport

- **C12:** Record lodging against the night following a travel day. Actual-cost lodging reimburses the personally paid allocated expense amount once. Employer-paid/provided lodging has zero lodging payout and cannot also receive a lodging allowance. For privately paid lodging using an eligible allowance, preserve the actual cost/evidence but exclude that actual cost from payable expenses. An eligible allowance uses that night's location and annual rate; provided lodging and in-transit accommodation receive no lodging allowance. Prevent reimbursement of both actual cost and an allowance for the same night; an informational invoice can still be retained. The last travel day has no following trip night. The inherited domestic allowance is EUR 20, supported by [R 9.7 LStR](https://lsth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/A-Einkommensteuergesetz/II-Einkommen-2-24b/4-Ueberschuss-d-Einnahmen-ueber-die-Werbungsk-8-9a/Paragraf-9/r-9-7.html). This guidance also excludes employer-provided lodging from tax-free lodging allowances.
- **C13:** Under actual-cost reimbursement, reimburse a personally paid hotel bill including breakfast in full and apply the provided-breakfast deduction to the following day. Never also deduct a fixed breakfast amount from the hotel bill. Propose the meal flag visibly and retain any correction. A company-paid breakfast can also affect the meal allowance even though the hotel invoice itself has zero payout.
- **C14:** Private-vehicle reimbursement equals total driven kilometers × applicable vehicle rate, rounded once to EUR cents. The inherited rates are EUR 0.30/km for cars and EUR 0.20/km for other motor vehicles. Verify their employer-reimbursement basis before release. Enter total distance explicitly; a round-trip helper doubles a one-way entry once. No commuting allowance, bicycle allowance or route service is included.

## Expenses, VAT and FX

- **C15:** Reimburse recorded eligible personally paid gross expenses, not their net amounts. Company-paid expenses are informational and have zero reimbursable amount; keeping their VAT data must not increase the payout. For lodging, apply the selected actual-cost/allowance/no-reimbursement method before adding totals. Allow explicitly unknown VAT without claiming input tax; unknown is distinct from a verified 0% rate. When VAT portions are recorded, they must sum to the expense gross amount; net + VAT must equal gross for each portion. An Eigenbeleg carries no deductible VAT. Never infer VAT from a meal/mileage/lodging allowance.
- **C16:** With a gross portion and rate in basis points, VAT = round(gross × rate / (10000 + rate)); net = gross − VAT. Accept invoice-stated net/VAT values instead, warn about deviations greater than one cent, and preserve stated amounts. Rate choices must be date-aware; never infer the tax treatment of food or lodging from cost type alone.
- **C17:** Show German and foreign VAT separately. A tax-country flag or employer-name match alone does not establish deductibility. Core exports report VAT amounts, invoice recipient/type and evidence gaps for employer review, rather than asserting legally deductible input VAT. Warn when the invoice recipient appears unsuitable under the applicable small-invoice rules; verify the threshold/rules before enabling that check.
- **C18:** Store FX as foreign currency units per EUR 1. Convert using decimal amounts and currency exponents: EUR amount = foreign amount / rate. Default to the ECB rate on the receipt date, otherwise the latest preceding publication within seven days. Without one, require a manual rate or actual EUR debit. Record source, publication date, override and original amount. Network failure must leave manual entry available. FX selection is an inherited product convention; VAT conversion may require different treatment and remains employer review.
- **C19:** Convert an expense total once. When converting VAT portions, distribute any residual cents deterministically so the EUR portions equal that total; retain net + VAT = gross. Preserve an actual card-debit amount exactly. Never reconstruct it from a rounded displayed implicit rate.
- **C20:** Hospitality reimburses the personally paid gross amount including documented tip. Record the tip separately with no claimed VAT. Capture the user's own meal explicitly and suggest a provided meal for review. The employer's 70/30 business-deductibility split is outside Core.

## Standalone outlays

**C22:** Tools/equipment, software/services and other business outlays use their expense date to select a claim period. Reimburse the eligible personally paid gross amount once, with the same VAT, FX and evidence rules as travel expenses. They create no travel days, meal allowance or mileage. Their business eligibility is user-declared; the app does not determine depreciation, asset capitalization or payroll tax treatment.

## Totals

**C21:** Trip total = payable actual travel expenses + meal allowance after deductions/exclusions + eligible lodging allowance + mileage allowance. Each component appears exactly once and is grouped by cost type. Standalone outlay total = sum of selected eligible personally paid outlays without a trip. Claim reimbursement total = sum of selected trip totals + standalone outlay total. Company-paid expenses and actual lodging costs replaced by an allowance contribute zero to these payable sums. Export actual paid cost, payer and reimbursable amount separately. Payout = reimbursement total − selected advances. Permit a negative payout, labeled repayment to the employer.

The Core does not certify that all reimbursed meal expenses are tax-free. Flag potentially taxable reimbursement and leave payroll treatment to the employer; acknowledgement must not change the tax characterization in an export.
